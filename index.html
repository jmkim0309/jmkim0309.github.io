AI Researcher at Kakao

Jongmin Kim

I’m an AI researcher at Kakao working on video generation and computer vision. My current research focuses on making image-to-video models faster and improving how they render people in motion. I use adversarial post-training to speed up generation and reinforcement learning with human feedback to reduce artifacts while preserving motion.

I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at KAIST in 2019. My doctoral research focused on graph neural networks for few-shot learning. At Kakao and KakaoBrain, my research has expanded to text-to-image generation, multimodal learning, and video generation.

Research projects

Video generation

Faster, more reliable image-to-video models

At Kakao, I work with diffusion and flow-matching models for image-to-video generation. I use adversarial post-training to reduce denoising steps and reinforcement learning with human feedback to reduce visual artifacts while preserving motion.
